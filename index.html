THE NOVEL

Darren Akito is a boy living in the quiet shadows of everyday life—unnoticed, uncomplicated, and largely forgotten by those around him.

That delicate balance fractures when a mysterious Fragment, hidden beneath his home, surfaces from the unknown. It was never meant to be touched, nor was it meant to awaken.

As subtle shifts begin to take root within him, Darren realizes that disturbing the silence comes with a cost: once you step into the dark, something in the dark begins to look back.

"Some things are not meant to be found."
