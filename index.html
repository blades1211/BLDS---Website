The Foundation

Before the world fractured, there was only the Core—an absolute anchor bound to the very essence of its bearer. A Core does not merely exist; it listens, reflects, and bends to the deepest qualities, convictions, and inner shadows of the one who holds it.

What remains scattered across the silent void are only Fragments.

"A Fragment is not the Core itself.
It is merely a piece of something far greater."

When awakening occurs, the energy manifests through dual natures. Flames arise as radiant manifestations born of conviction, willpower, and alignment with one’s noble truth. Yet, where light takes form, Shadow Flames inevitably follow—darker counterparts forged from repressed anger, fear, and unresolved trauma.

For Darren Akito, who spent his youth existing as a forgotten ghost in the shadows, discovering the cold shard buried beneath his room was not an accident. It was an awakening to a force that had been waiting for someone truly unseen.
