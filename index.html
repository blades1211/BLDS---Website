THE NOVEL

♦
CHAPTER I ♦

THE UNSEEN BOY

Darren Akito is a boy almost nobody notices. Ignored at school, invisible at home—until he discovers something hidden beneath the floor of his house. A black Fragment. And when he touches it... something notices him.

CHAPTER II ♦

THE AWAKENING

Something inside Darren has changed. His reflexes, his senses, his body. Damian Charles Whitmore begins to notice that Darren is no longer an easy target. Meanwhile, the Fragment’s presence becomes harder to ignore.

CHAPTER III ♦

THE VOICE BEYOND DARKNESS

Darren begins hearing something he cannot explain. Inside a strange black void, he encounters Kael Vireon. Kael knows about the Fragment, but he refuses to reveal everything.

CHAPTER IV ♦

THE CORE

Something deeper begins forming inside Darren. The Fragment was only the beginning. A strange energy takes shape—The Core. But power does not come without consequence.

CHAPTER V ♦

THE FIRST PURSUIT

Darren is no longer unnoticed. Someone has found him. What begins as a normal walk home turns into a pursuit. Kael appears with a dire warning: "They have found you."
